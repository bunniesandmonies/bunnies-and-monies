Privacy policy

Bunnies & Monies, by Learning Monies Pty Ltd. Last updated 9 November 2026.

In short: Bunnies & Monies is made for children. It does not collect, store, use or share any personal information, and it never connects to the internet.

What we do not collect

  • No names, ages, birthdays, email addresses, photos, voice recordings or drawings.
  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone access.
  • No advertising identifiers or device identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking, cookies or crash reporting.

What the game does not include

  • No advertising of any kind.
  • No links to other websites or apps.
  • No chat, messaging or contact with other people.
  • No purchases with real money. The coins in the game are pretend.

Game progress saved on the device

To remember how far a child has got, the game saves its progress (coins, jars, treats, surprises and hearts) on the device itself. This stays on the device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it does not identify the child. A grown-up can clear it at any time: on the title screen, tap the lightbulb, answer the grown-up question, then press and hold "start the game again from zero". Deleting the app also removes it.

The voice

All spoken words are recordings built into the game. Nothing a child says or does is recorded or sent anywhere.

Grown-ups area

Tips for grown-ups, this privacy policy and the option to start again are behind a "grown-ups only" question, so young children don't reach them by accident.

Children's privacy laws

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it is designed to comply with children's privacy laws, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States, the GDPR and UK GDPR, and the Australian Privacy Principles.

Third parties

The game shares no information with any third party. The app stores (Apple App Store and Google Play) handle downloads under their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top before releasing the change.

Contact us

Questions about privacy? Contact Learning Monies Pty Ltd at admin@learningmonies.com.au.
