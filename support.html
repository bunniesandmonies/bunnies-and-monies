What is Bunnies & Monies?

A gentle money game for young children, based on the book The Carrot Coin Mystery. Children earn carrot coins by helping around Bunny Village, then choose to save, spend or give them. There are no timers, no losing and no ads.

Common questions

How do I start again from zero?
On the title screen, tap the small lightbulb, answer the grown-up question, then press and hold "start the game again from zero".

What is the grown-up question?
A short multiplication question written in words. It keeps the grown-ups area away from young children.

There's no sound.
Turn the volume up, and on an iPhone or iPad check that Silent Mode is off.

Does the game need the internet?
No. It works fully offline and never connects to the internet.

Is my child's information collected?
No. See the privacy policy (privacy.html on this site).
